Movement with Meaning

We’re building a welcoming third place where people connect and grow.

Why we exist

Kurious is a multi‑sport movement facility designed as a modern “third place” — a social hub beyond home and work where people of all ages can train, socialise, and grow together.

We bring bouldering, parkour & ninja, and a strength gym together under one membership, fostering real connection through shared challenge and play.

What makes us different

  • Diverse disciplines under a single membership for unmatched value.
  • Intentionally designed for social connection, youth development, and long‑term community engagement.
  • Lower pricing than typical bouldering gyms while offering far more than a standard gym.

How we build community

  • Classes for all levels to learn, progress, and feel supported.
  • Regular events — from movie nights to competitions — to keep things exciting.
  • A community hub & coffee bar where you can just hang out and belong.

Our pillars

  • Parkour + Ninja: inclusive, non‑hierarchical coaching and playful movement.
  • Bouldering: confidence through problem‑solving with a welcoming culture.
  • Strength: functional training with built‑in accountability and support networks.
